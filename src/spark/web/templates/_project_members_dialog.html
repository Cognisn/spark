Members

Share this project with people on this instance. Project and all conversations is live: every conversation in the project, now and later and whoever started it, is visible to that person. Changing someone to Project only returns those conversations to private for whoever started them, except those shared one by one.

Add a person

Choose a person from the results to add them with the role and scope above.
Transfer ownership

The new owner takes over this project, its sub-projects and resources, with their storage use. You stay as a Contributor with all conversations; conversations stay with whoever started them. The transfer is refused if the new owner's storage cannot hold the resources. A person who is not yet a member is added in the same step.
